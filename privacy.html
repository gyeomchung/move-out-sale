Privacy policy

This is a personal move-out sale run by Gyeom Chung in Brooklyn, NY.

What we collect. Only what you type into the site: questions you post (shown publicly under an anonymous guest name), and, when you reserve a pickup or join a waitlist, your name and contact (phone or email).

How it's used. Your contact is used only to arrange your pickup or tell you an item is available. It is visible only to the seller, stored in a private Google Sheet, and never sold or shared.

Retention. All reservation and waitlist data is deleted after the sale ends (December 2026).

Google sign-in. The site's Google integration is used only by the seller to run the sale; visitors never sign in.

Questions or deletion requests: use the “Ask” button on any item, or reply to the seller's message.
